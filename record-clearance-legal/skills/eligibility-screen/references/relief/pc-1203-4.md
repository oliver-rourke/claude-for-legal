# PC 1203.4: dismissal after probation

**Screened in v0.1 and v0.2: yes.** Record fields used: `probation_granted`, `probation_outcome`, `code_section`, `status.current_supervision`, `status.pending_case`, `status.registration_290`.

## What it is

The main California dismissal statute for convictions that ended in a grant of probation. When its conditions are met the court permits the person to withdraw a guilty or no-contest plea and enter a not-guilty plea, or sets aside a guilty verdict, and "shall thereupon dismiss the accusations or information," after which the person "shall thereafter be released from all penalties and disabilities resulting from the offense," with the exceptions the section lists (subd. (a)(1)).

## Who may qualify

Three routes, all in subdivision (a)(1):

- **Mandatory, probation fulfilled:** the person "has fulfilled the conditions of probation for the entire period of probation."
- **Mandatory, early discharge:** the person "has been discharged prior to the termination of the period of probation."
- **Discretionary:** "in any other case in which a court, in its discretion and the interest of justice, determines that a defendant should be granted the relief."

In every route the person must apply "at any time after the termination of the period of probation" and must not then be "serving a sentence for an offense, on probation for an offense, or charged with the commission of an offense" (subd. (a)(1)). The application may be made "in person or by attorney, or by the probation officer authorized in writing" (subd. (a)(1)).

An unfulfilled restitution order or restitution fine is not a ground for denial and "shall not be grounds for finding that a defendant did not fulfil the condition of probation for the entire period of probation" (subd. (c)(3)(A) to (C)).

## Who is excluded

Subdivision (a) "does not apply to a misdemeanor that is within the provisions of Section 42002.1 of the Vehicle Code, to a violation of subdivision (c) of Section 286, Section 288, subdivision (c) of Section 287 or of former Section 288a, Section 288.5, subdivision (j) of Section 289, Section 311.1, 311.2, 311.3, or 311.11, or a felony conviction pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 261.5, or to an infraction" (subd. (b)). Certain Vehicle Code offenses listed in Vehicle Code section 12810(a) to (e) are also outside subdivision (a) unless the court grants relief in its discretion (subd. (c)(1), (c)(2)).

## What the relief does and does not do

- In any later prosecution "the prior conviction may be pleaded and proved and shall have the same effect as if probation had not been granted or the accusation or information dismissed" (subd. (a)(1)).
- The person must still disclose the conviction "in response to any direct question contained in any questionnaire or application for public office, for licensure by any state or local agency, or for contracting with the California State Lottery Commission" (subd. (a)(1)).
- Dismissal "does not permit a person to own, possess, or have custody or control of a firearm" (subd. (a)(2)) and does not restore eligibility to hold public office where the conviction barred it (subd. (a)(3)).
- Unexpired criminal protective orders remain in effect (subd. (a)(4)).
- Vehicle Code section 13555 (driving privilege consequences) is carved out (subd. (a)(1)).

## Procedure notes

- "Relief shall not be granted under this section unless the prosecuting attorney has been given 15 days' notice of the petition for relief"; the probation officer notifies the prosecuting attorney when a petition is filed; proof of service creates a presumption of notice (subd. (d)).
- If the prosecuting attorney, after notice, "fails to appear and object," the prosecutor "may not move to set aside or otherwise appeal the grant of that petition" (subd. (e)).
- The probation papers must inform the person of this right and of any right to petition for a certificate of rehabilitation and pardon (subd. (a)(1)).

## Facts the screen needs

Whether probation was granted; whether it was completed, terminated early, revoked, or is ongoing; the code section if known (for the subdivision (b) check); and the client-level answers on supervision, pending charges and registration. The screen cannot tell from an intake whether a revoked-then-reinstated probation counts as fulfilled; that is an attorney call under the discretionary route.

## Plain-language explanation

If you were put on probation for a conviction and you finished it, you can usually ask the court to dismiss that conviction. If you finished probation without problems, or the court let you off early, the court must grant it. If probation was revoked at some point, the judge decides. You cannot be on probation, serving a sentence, or facing a new charge when you ask. A dismissal helps with most private job applications and housing, but you still have to disclose the conviction when you apply for a government job or a professional license, it does not give you back the right to own a gun, and the conviction can still count against you if you are ever charged again. Some kinds of convictions, mostly serious sex offenses against children and some driving offenses, cannot be dismissed this way.
