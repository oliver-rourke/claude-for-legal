# County filing guide: forms and procedure for dismissal petitions

Guidance for the attorney and staff after a screen. It lists the forms a county uses for a PC 1203.4, 1203.4a or 1203.41 petition, how the county serves the prosecutor, and the local practice points a filer needs. It does not fill or file anything.

**Provenance.** Imported 2026-10-04 from The Access Project's county filing tables (the forms table and the filing guide, one row per California county). Forms are Judicial Council forms unless a county version is named; revision years are as recorded. Local practice changes without notice: before filing, confirm the forms and the service address on the court's website and with the prosecutor's office. Tag anything copied from this file `[county filing guide, imported 2026-10-04 — verify with the court]`.

**How the screen uses it.** For each item on a petition route (a LIKELY ELIGIBLE item, or a NEEDS ATTORNEY REVIEW item on a discretionary route), add one "Filing in [county]" bullet under Next steps: the petition and order forms, the proof-of-service form, who is served and how, the declaration form when a declaration is usually needed, and one local point if the county has one (hearing date before filing, wet signature, copies, e-filing). The full county section goes out only when someone asks for the filing checklist. Read only the row and the section for the county in question.

**Any county, and counties not in the table.** The petition is Judicial Council form CR-180 (Petition for Dismissal, which covers PC 1203.4, 1203.4a, 1203.41, 1203.42 and 1203.49) and the proposed order is CR-181. Attach an MC-031 declaration when relief is discretionary or the county's standard set lists it. Serve the prosecuting attorney at least 15 days before relief (PC 1203.4(d)(1), PC 1203.41(e); PC 1203.4a states a notice period only for infractions) using CR-106 for service by mail or POS-050 for electronic service, as the court's local practice directs. Fire camp relief under PC 1203.4b uses CR-430 (petition), CR-431 (agency certification) and CR-432 (order). Proposition 64 uses CR-400, CR-402 and CR-403; PC 653.29 uses CR-425 and CR-426; Proposition 47 forms vary by county. Those three are not screened by this plugin; the forms are listed for the attorney's reference only.

## County notes

Court and prosecutor contact details are institutional, not personal, and are reproduced as recorded; confirm them before serving. Courthouse lists longer than six are summarized by count.

### Alameda
- **Preparation:** File P47/64/any speciality petitions separately from general CR-180 petition for dismissal
- **Conformed copy:** posted on the e-filing system after the hearing
- **E-filing:** cases prior to 2000 may not be available in e-filing system. In this case call the Alameda Clerk's office to request that they add the case to their e-filing system and get the new case number they assign to it. There are several options for looking up cases; by name, courtroom, judicial officer, etc. If case is not available on e-filing platform, has to be paper filed.
- **Case lookup:** https://publicportal.alameda.courts.ca.gov/publicportal/ (registration required)
- **Case lookup note:** There are several options for looking up cases; by name, courtroom, judicial officer, etc.
- **Court:** https://www.alameda.courts.ca.gov
- **Courthouses:** Dublin - East County Hall of Justice, Fremont Hall of Justice, Oakland - Wiley W. Manuel Courthouse
- **Prosecutor offices:** Alameda
- **DA service email:** alcoda@acgov.org

### Alpine
- **Case lookup note:** Alpine County Superior Court does not have an online search engine that allows for public access to the court's case management system for Name Searches and Records. All search requests and copying are performed by a clerk or staff member employed by the court.
- **Court:** https://www.alpine.courts.ca.gov
- **Courthouses:** Alpine County Superior Court
- **Prosecutor offices:** Alpine
