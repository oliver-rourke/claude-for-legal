California record clearance law has been amended every year since 2019, and two of the sections below moved from petition-based relief to automatic Department of Justice relief in 2024. Before relying on a waiting period, an exclusion, or an operative date, verify it against the current text. Each row names the last amendment the fetched text showed and where to verify.

## Things that move without a statute changing

- **Appropriations.** Automatic relief under PC 1203.425 and PC 851.93 is "subject to an appropriation in the annual Budget Act." A missing "relief granted" note can mean the program was paused, not that the person is ineligible. Check the Department of Justice's OpenJustice statistics (PC 1203.425(a)(6)) when a batch of records shows no notes.
- **Judicial Council forms.** Petition and order forms are revised; the plugin does not ship form numbers for that reason.
- **Local practice.** Service methods, hearing practice and declaration expectations vary by county and by judge; the profile's `## Jurisdiction` section is the place to record them.
